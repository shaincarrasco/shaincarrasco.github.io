Climb Intel

Founder and sole developer · live product

An app that grades Kilter Board routes and predicts how hard a climb will feel for your body, using your height, wingspan and reach. You can also upload a video of yourself climbing and get feedback on each move.

  • 72.8% of grades within ±1 V-grade, ~90% within ±2
  • 137,000 graded routes used for training
  • Tracks 17 body joints from phone video with MediaPipe

Scoring AI telescope schedules

Research · SkAI Institute, summer 2026

An AI telescope scheduler needs a way to tell a good night of observing from a bad one. I built a scoring toolkit for this and tested it on 105,890 real Dark Energy Survey exposures.

  • Calibrated effective exposure time matches the DES pipeline at 0.952 log-correlation
  • Scored 615 real survey nights
  • Found a Simpson's paradox: scheduler logs make seeing look better at high airmass, which is physically backwards
