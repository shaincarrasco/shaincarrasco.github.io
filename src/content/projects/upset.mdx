## How the number is made

Each real NFL game is simulated 10,000 times with total and spread drawn around the sportsbook line, so a QB and his receivers rise and fall together and a team protecting a lead runs more. Game scripts become player stat lines, league scoring turns them into P(win), margin, total and "what moves this."

## Under the hood (private `matchup-market`)

A monorepo: a seeded odds engine with calibration and backtest; an LMSR house market maker with a double-entry ledger in integer centicoins and idempotency keys; a Sleeper connector (username → leagues → rosters → matchups) plus ESPN lines and live stats; a Neon Function for the shared league board; and a Discord/GroupMe odds-posting bot (dry run by default).

Play coins only. Not purchasable, not redeemable, no real-money bets.
