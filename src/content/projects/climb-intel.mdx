## What it is

The official Kilter Board app knows hundreds of thousands of routes but nothing about *you*. Climb Intel grades routes with machine learning, adjusts difficulty for your height, wingspan and reach, analyzes climbing videos for movement coaching, and recommends routes near your limit.

## How the grade is made

A stacked **XGBoost + LightGBM → meta-learner → isotonic calibration** ensemble, trained on about 137,000 graded Kilter routes. On a frozen, leakage-free holdout: 72.8% within ±1 V-grade, ~90% within ±2, ~29% exact. Strongest at V4–V6; V9+ is the open problem because data is scarce at the top.

## Video coaching

Upload a phone clip. MediaPipe extracts 17 body joints per frame; each move gets feedback on tension, hip angle, reach and footwork, benchmarked against per-grade medians.

## The product

~24 pages in a vanilla-JS PWA behind a Flask API with 13 blueprints (auth, social, routes, predict, pose, billing…) and a PostgreSQL pool. Real accounts with bcrypt and Google OAuth, paid subscriptions through Stripe (Climber Pro, $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a 7-day trial), offline-capable, installable. Push to `main` and Railway redeploys climbintel.com.

Full system design: [climbintel.com/architecture.html](https://climbintel.com/architecture.html).
