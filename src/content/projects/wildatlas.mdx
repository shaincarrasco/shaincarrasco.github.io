## The big pieces

1. **The globe** — MapLibre GL JS globe with 3D terrain, dark-neon style, land cover, forest types, biomes from PMTiles.
2. **The data** — GBIF and iNaturalist occurrences, IUCN threat status and climate niches, aggregated to H3 hexes in Neon Postgres/PostGIS.
3. **The papers** — OpenAlex, PMC, Plazi and BHL mined for species, places and expedition findings; every pin links to its source.
4. **The people** — profiles, life lists, video sightings that feed the map, endangered watchlists.
5. **The mission** — geoprivacy for sensitive species, data shared back to GBIF, recovery and rediscovery signals.

Built with a collaborator on a 10-phase plan with architecture decision records, a data-source license register, and Playwright smoke tests against live data. Working codename; final name pending.
