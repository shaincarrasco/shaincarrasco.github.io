- **Film Room** — pose-track your jumper, frame scrubber with ghost reference, mechanics radar, drill-based coaching plan.
- **Tactical Board** — tap-to-build lineup picker scored over every five-man combination, pairwise synergy heatmap, what-if swaps and trade scenarios, three-model lab.
- **NBA//DESK** — leak-free rolling form + Elo → calibrated win probabilities; slate picker, power rankings, Monte Carlo playoff odds, upset detector.

Runs offline on realistic sample data; an optional `nba_api` loader pulls real data. Each demo writes a self-contained `report.html` with no CDN.
