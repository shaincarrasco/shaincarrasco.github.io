## How it works

1. The photo gets a quality check.
2. Classical segmentation separates pieces from the floor: a floor color model, shadow trimming, seam cuts.
3. Studs set the 8 mm scale.
4. Footprint, stud pattern and a height cue identify the part; CIELAB/CIEDE2000 matching identifies the color.
5. A calibrated confidence per piece decides whether it is confirmed automatically or goes to a two-second check tray.
6. Only verified scans reach your inventory and exports (Rebrickable CSV, BrickLink wanted list).
