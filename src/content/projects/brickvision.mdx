## How it works

Photo in with a quality check → classical segmentation (floor color model, shadow trimming, seam cuts) → studs set the 8 mm scale → footprint, stud pattern and height cue give the part; CIELAB/CIEDE2000 matching gives the color → calibrated confidence per piece decides whether it is auto-confirmed or goes to a 2-second **Check** tray → only verified scans reach your inventory and exports (Rebrickable CSV, BrickLink wanted list).
