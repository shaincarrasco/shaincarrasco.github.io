An fsynced, CRC-framed journal is the only state; a start gate means no job runs without a durable record. Jobs that place orders never retry or catch up on their own. Two weeks of missed fires handled in one 0.5 s tick. Four high-severity findings from a separate review fixed, each with a regression test. v0.1.0.
