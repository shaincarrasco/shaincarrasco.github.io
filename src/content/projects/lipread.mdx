## What it does

Open the link, add it to your home screen, pick or record a clip. The model runs in the browser; reading starts immediately and shows progress. Type the names you expect (players, coaches) and the app checks for them.

## Status

Pre-release. The phone app has been checked in desktop Chromium at phone size; real-iPhone and Android testing is next. A desktop app (v0.1) has measured results in the repo's `eval/`. Ships with `ETHICS.md`, `PRIVACY.md` and model licenses.
